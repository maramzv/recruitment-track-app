Candidate Tracker

Hello, Recruiter! 👋

Welcome to Candidate Tracker.

Built to help you juggle multiple candidates at once. If you've ever lost track of who you last followed up with or let a promising candidate go cold, this tool keeps it visible.

  • ✅ Add candidates and track their hiring stage
  • ✅ Log your last contact date
  • ✅ Get flagged automatically when someone's overdue for a follow-up

⚠️ Delete Candidate?

Workforce Analytics (Planned)

Chart-based, concise breakdown of the historical Kaggle workforce dataset — read-only, not part of the live candidate pipeline.

  • Recruitment source effectiveness
  • Department overview
  • Employee status / retention
  • Performance & satisfaction averages
  • Turnover reasons

Hiring Intelligence (Planned)

Cross-referencing live pipeline candidates with historical workforce dataset metrics.

  • Department insights when a candidate's department is selected
  • Recruitment source recommendations based on historical outcomes
  • When a candidate is marked Hired, contributes a new record here alongside historical data
