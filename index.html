ℹ️ General Recruiter Note

Use historical signals as conversation guides, not candidate verdicts.

Source and department patterns describe what has happened historically; they do not determine how an individual candidate will perform or how long they will stay.

These signals should help recruiters ask better questions and identify areas worth validating, not determine whether a candidate is qualified or should move forward.

Historical signals should determine what you investigate, not what you conclude.
